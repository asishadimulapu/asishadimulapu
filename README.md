## About me

<img align="right" width="300" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abhisheknaiidu/abhisheknaiidu/master/code.gif" alt="Animated illustration of a developer at a desk"/>

B.Tech in AI & ML at **Anurag University**.

I like taking ML research and turning it into something people can actually use: an API, a web app, a tool that answers questions over real documents. Most of my work sits where LLMs, computer vision and full-stack development meet.

- 🔭 **Building:** [LegalGpt](https://github.com/asishadimulapu/LegalGpt), a RAG system for legal document analysis
- 🌱 **Learning:** AWS, Kubernetes and cloud-native architecture
- 💬 **Ask me about:** RAG pipelines, Gemini API, ML model deployment, React + Node/Flask backends
- 🤝 **Open to:** AI/ML and full-stack roles and internships
